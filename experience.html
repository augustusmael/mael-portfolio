01 How I got here

Started on chat. Stayed for the systems.

I started in BPO the same way most people do — on the floor, handling high-volume customer interactions through chat, and getting graded on it every day. That grading is what pulled me in: I wanted to know why some responses scored well and others didn't, and what made a team's numbers move.

That curiosity is what took me from Customer Service Representative to Subject Matter Expert. Today I split my time between two things: keeping quality scores high across a live campaign, and building the reports, SOPs, and calibration sessions that make those scores repeatable instead of lucky.

It's the same instinct that shows up in the automation and reporting work on the Services page — I like operations that explain themselves.
